About SOGI: Solution of Glass & Industries

A Nepali manufacturer of architectural glass hardware based in Bharatpur, Chitwan. We also sell glass and mirrors, and SOGI Projects installs glass work across Nepal.

Contact us
Who we are

Glass hardware, made in Nepal

SOGI was started to give Nepal's glass industry a reliable local source of quality glass hardware, so fabricators and contractors don't have to depend only on imports. From our head office and factory in Bharatpur, Chitwan, we manufacture stainless steel glass fittings and supply more than 1,200 hardware products to fabricators, contractors, architects, interior designers, wholesalers and retailers across Nepal.

Our fittings are made in SS304 and SS202 mirror-finish stainless steel. Connectors, patch fittings, handles, shower hinges, spigots, spider and canopy fittings are drilled, ground, polished and welded by our own team. We also sell toughened, laminated, DGU and fluted glass, mirrors and LED mirrors, sourced from trusted glass makers. We don't manufacture glass ourselves.

Our newest division, SOGI Projects, uses SOGI hardware and premium toughened glass to deliver finished glass work for homes, offices, hotels, showrooms, banks, hospitals and schools.
