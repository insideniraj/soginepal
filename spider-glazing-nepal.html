Overview

Spider Glazing & Structural Glass

Spider glazing holds large glass panels at their corners with stainless steel spider fittings and articulated routels, giving a clean, frameless glass wall. It is used for hotel lobbies, showroom fronts, entrance canopies and double-height spaces.

SOGI manufactures and supplies spider fittings (1-, 2- and 4-arm), routels and fin plates. SOGI Projects installs spider glazing on suitable jobs. Larger facade and curtain-wall projects are taken on request, after a technical review.

Who it's for

  • Hotel lobbies
  • Showroom fronts
  • Commercial entrances
  • Banks & corporate offices
  • Hospitals
  • Colleges

How it's built

  1. 4-arm spider fitting
  2. Toughened glass panel
  3. Glass fin or steel support behind
  4. 2-arm spider at the edge
  5. Structural silicone joint
  6. Floor or slab line

Design options: Glass: clear, reflective, tinted, laminated, DGU · System: spider on steel frame or glass fin

How we make sure it lasts

  • Spider glazing needs engineering: wind load, glass size, deflection and fixing design are checked for every job.
  • Height work needs scaffolding or lifting equipment and a trained team.
  • We work with architects, builders and contractors on these projects.
  • Spider fittings and routels are SOGI-made stainless steel.

FAQ

Spider Glazing & Structural Glass: questions

What is spider glazing?

A glazing system where glass panels are held at the corners by stainless steel 'spider' fittings fixed to a steel frame or glass fins, with no frame around the glass.

Do you do full building facades?

Facade and curtain-wall work is taken on request after a technical review of the project. Contact SOGI Projects with your drawings.

Can I buy spider fittings only?

Yes. SOGI supplies spider fittings, articulated routels and fin plates wholesale. Call +977-9705663388.
